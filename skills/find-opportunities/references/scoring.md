# Fit Scoring (0 to 100)

Score against the owner's offer map in `config/profile.md`, never against a fixed profession. Be honest: do not round up to make a post look better.

## Skill match (0 to 35)
- 30 to 35: the need maps to capabilities marked `strong` and in the owner's current focus
- 15 to 29: partial match, or only `strong` capabilities outside the current focus
- 5 to 14: only `light` capabilities
- 0: only `none` capabilities (do not show the post)

## Engagement type (0 to 15)
- 15: a type the owner wants
- 10: a type the owner is open to
- 0: a type the owner is not interested in (also a skip)

## Eligibility (0 to 15)
- 15: no restriction, or restriction the owner meets (location, time zone, language, authorisation)
- 8: not stated
- 0: restriction the owner cannot meet (skip regardless of score)

## Decision-maker and clarity (0 to 15)
- Poster is founder, owner, team lead, or hiring manager: +8
- Scope, timeline, or tools stated: +7

## Freshness and visibility (0 to 15)
- Under 12 hours: 15; under 24h: 12; under 48h: 8; under 5 days: 3
- Subtract 3 if over 80 comments, 6 if over 150 (the comment will be buried)

## Budget signal (0 to 5)
- Clear paid scope or rate stated: 5
- Not stated: 2

## Deductions
- Soft red flag from red-flags.md: -8 each
- Vague scope with large expectations: -8

## Bands
- 80 and above: strong, draft first and comment early
- 60 to 79: decent, draft if time allows
- Below 60: not shown
