# Register Examples (style only, from different professions)

These show length, directness, and warmth. They are from varied fields on purpose. Never copy the words; rebuild every line from the actual post and the owner's own offer map and proof points.

## Direct offer (Option A style)
"A two-week handover plan usually saves more time than people expect, and I have set these up for finance teams before. Happy to look at what you have and suggest where to start. Want me to send a short example by message?"

## Question-led (Option B style)
"Quick question on the rebrand: is the goal a full visual system or just the logo and a few templates? That changes how I would scope it. I work with early-stage founders on this and can share two samples if useful."

## Partial fit, honest
"I can cover the research and the first draft of the report well. The data visualisation part I would leave to a specialist. If the writing can be split out, I am glad to talk."

## Fresh post, short
"Onboarding calls that run long often come down to the first five minutes. I coach customer success teams on exactly this and can tell you quickly what is worth changing. Shall I message you a short outline?"

## DM follow-up (draft only)
"Hi [Name], I commented on your post about [need]. Short version: I can help with [part], and I can start [availability line]. If you share a bit more about [one detail], I will reply with a quick plan."

## What to avoid
- Opening with praise ("Great post")
- Listing ten skills
- Saying "I am passionate" or "I would love the opportunity"
- Dropping a price or a link in a public comment (unless config allows)
- Claiming results, clients, or tools that are not in the owner's profile
